About Me

PhD Candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Edinburgh, specializing in geophysical fluid dynamics. My expertise includes Lagrangian averaging within the Generalised Lagrangian Mean (GLM) framework, geometric formulation of fluid models, asymptotic analysis, wave–mean flow interaction, and computational methods using Fourier pseudo-spectral solvers.

Email: abhijeet.minz@ED.ac.uk
